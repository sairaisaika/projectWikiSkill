## Method implications

Use the native Wiki to route the actual edit seam and consumers; DTO presence does not close the consumption edge. Reconcile accepted product intent with current declarations and implementation, and keep prospective source separate from the running artifact. Treat success-looking scripts and package-executor selection as evidence questions. Keep actual runtime, test manifests, independent review and product acceptance separate rather than adding global reading or compliance gates.
