## Where cooperation actually stopped

Claude's companion R3 had returned source and a terminal receipt. Root had not
integrated or tested the product page, then moved to wiki tooling. The work was
returned but not adopted. This is an ownership/continuation gap as well as a
documentation opportunity; it is not evidence that a whole wiki failed or that
an agent's terminal success equals the user's result.

For the next native handoff, record current owner, actual stage, who received the
return, next action and remaining evidence in the existing task log. Do not create
a parallel business ledger or derive lifecycle state from the existence of a file.
Distinguish prepared packet, actual dispatch, delivered context, worker return,
coordinator review, source integration, runtime verification and release. A child
can recommend a design; the parent must say what it adopted/rejected and why.
